About The commerical Sangria Boulevard

People go shopping looking for more than just the joy of a purchase. They seek an experience, a fulfillment, an indulgence. The Boulevard @ Marvel Ganga Sangria promises to give all that, and more. Designed as a pedestrian mall, the retail spaces have luxury brand shops spilling on the street. Planned to offer ample visibility to all stores, footfalls are a given. Whether it’s designer stores, bookshops, cafes, or restaurants, The Boulevard @ Marvel Ganga Sangria puts out an impressive spread of exclusive internationally-renowned names for your consumers to feast on.
