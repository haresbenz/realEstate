, Beach Villas start from AED 29.34M for a 6 bedroom and AED 30.81M for a 5 bedroom. Coral Villas start from AED 47.90M for a 6 bedroom and AED 49.39M for a 7 bedroom. These are the official launch starting prices and are subject to availability at the time of booking.

Palm Jebel Ali sits off the coast of Dubai between Jebel Ali and Dubai Marina, roughly 10 minutes from Ibn Battuta Mall, about 22 minutes from Dubai Marina and Al Maktoum International Airport, and around 38 minutes from Downtown Dubai.
Estimated construction completion is January 2030.

Palm Jebel Ali is developed by Nakheel, the master developer behind Palm Jumeirah,
The World Islands and Dubai Islands, now part of Dubai Holding.

Palm Jebel Ali is roughly twice the size of Palm Jumeirah. It is planned with 16 fronds and two crescent islands, adding approximately 110 km of coastline and housing an estimated 35,000 families.

Yes. Palm Jebel Ali is a designated freehold area, so buyers of any nationality can own a villa outright in their own name.

A UAE property investment of AED 2M or more can qualify the owner for a 10-year Golden Visa, subject to the ICP rules in force at the time of application. Every villa is well above that threshold.
