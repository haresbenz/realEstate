About The Residental PIAZZA

Marvel Piazza at Viman Nagar in Pune, is inspired by the open plazas of charming European towns, where picturesque courtyards connect the different wings of the project. Buy Flats in Pune from the Top Real Estate Company in Pune, our luxury residential properties come with unmatched quality and new-age design.

Choose from any of the New 2, 3 & 4 BHK apartments in Pune, and you will choose more than just a home. A life unbound by rules or bricks, a home at Marvel Piazza stretches beyond its carpet area. The open-air plazas embedded between the apartment blocks allow you to embrace the wind, sunshine, and trees.

Marvel Piazza takes you back to a simpler time when communities not just shared common spaces but bonded in equal measure. Because like the open plazas of charming European towns, you'll find picturesque courtyards connecting the different wings of Piazza. Wings that house one of 2, 3, 3.5 & 4.5 BHK apartments & penthouses that you could call home. So that all you need to reconnect with the hidden dimensions of your personality, and connect with like-minded souls in the process, is step out.

CONNECTIONS BEYOND CARPET AREA

Open-air plazas. Connecting courtyards with elegant sidewalks lined with trees. Glittering pools and verdant decks. Just a snapshot of the exteriors at Piazza. The open areas in the campus are free of any vehicular movement, and present a safe, green haven for children and families. In fact, a single sprawl of basement, from where each of the blocks is directly accessible, has been assigned solely for vehicles. So when you are outdoors, nothing comes in the way of building new bonds or uncovering new layers of your persona.

STATE-OF-THE-ART HOME AUTOMATION

Comfort, convenience and security. With Marvel’s state-of-the-art home automation, you are in charge of every gadget, every corner of your home.

Automated Apartment Management Systems, with touch screens / remote controls, let’s you control your entire house from centrally located LCD touch panel. Access control in the lift lobby and 24-hour video security make living in a Marvel home safe for your loved ones.

LIVING RESPONSIBLY

Living in a Marvel home means you are automatically living a clean, pure and natural lifestyle. Various good practices and our core values will enable you to limit your carbon footprint, while continuing to live in lavish comfort.

Through several green initiatives that are incorporated during construction, as well as specifications within the home that are committed to the planet, at Marvel, we make an environmentally sustainable lifestyle a reality.
